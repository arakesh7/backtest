Category 1: Capital, Margin & Cash-Flow Discrepancies (The "Phantom Capital" Trap)
CRIT-01 Unconstrained Short Selling & Artificial Cash Generation
Critical Solvency Flaw

Root Cause & Structural Flaw

In broker.py (lines 119-151), fund availability checks exist only for BUY orders. When a strategy executes a SELL order to initiate a short position:

  • Zero cash or margin checks are performed.
  • The broker credits raw cash directly: self.cash += (fill_size * fill_price) - commission.
  • No initial margin (e.g. 50% Reg T or 20% span) is escrowed.
  • No maintenance margin or short borrow interest is charged.

Quantitative & Risk Impact

An account with $1,000 can short-sell $10,000,000 worth of stock. The backtester immediately inflates self.cash to $10,001,000! The strategy is then permitted to deploy this phantom cash to purchase $10M worth of long assets.

Any Long/Short equity, statistical arbitrage, or market-neutral strategy tested on this engine will operate with infinite unconstrained leverage.

broker.py — Lines 119–151 Unchecked Short Proceeds 
# broker.py line 120: Funds checked ONLY for BUY orders
if order.side == "BUY":
    cost = (fill_size * fill_price) + commission
    if cost > self.cash:
        order.status = "REJECTED"
        return None

# broker.py line 147: SELL order unconditionally adds unencumbered cash
if order.side == "BUY":
    self.cash -= (fill_size * fill_price) + commission
else:  # SELL (both long exit AND short initiation!)
    self.cash += (fill_size * fill_price) - commission  # <-- Free cash injection without margin
Real-World Banking Failure Scenario

A quant modeller builds an equity pairs trading strategy with $5,000,000 seed allocation. In the backtest, whenever the spread widens, the model shorts 100,000 shares of an overpriced stock, receiving $20M in cash, and buys the underpriced pair. The backtest reports a flawless Sharpe 3.2. When deployed live, Prime Brokerage rejects the short order due to margin requirements (or issues an intraday margin call during adverse mark-to-market), forcing liquidations and generating catastrophic losses.

CRIT-02 Fixed Risk Sizer Inverts Risk Formula & Sizes on Depleting Cash
Critical Risk Flaw

Root Cause & Structural Flaw

In sizer.py (lines 48-49), FixedRiskPercentSizer ignores its own stop_loss_price parameter:

  • Formula used: int(cash_to_risk / entry_price).
  • This is Capital Allocation (allocating 1% of cash), NOT Risk Sizing!
  • Institutional Risk Sizing requires sizing according to stop distance: cash_to_risk / abs(entry_price - stop_loss_price).
  • Sizes against broker.cash rather than Total Portfolio NAV.

Quantitative & Risk Impact

If a modeller specifies a 1% portfolio risk with a tight $0.50 stop on a $100 stock (0.5% price risk), the sizer severely undersizes the trade.

Conversely, if the stop is $50 (50% price risk), the sizer buys the exact same number of shares, taking on 100x more tail risk than configured! As cash is deployed, bet sizes decay exponentially.

class FixedRiskPercentSizer(Sizer):

        cash_to_risk = broker.cash * (self.risk_percent / 100)
        return int(cash_to_risk / entry_price)  # <-- Divides by price, NOT risk per share!

# Institutional Standard Fix:
        risk_per_share = abs(entry_price - stop_loss_price)
        portfolio_equity = broker.cash + sum(p.size * last_close for p in broker.positions.values())
        return int((portfolio_equity * (self.risk_percent / 100)) / risk_per_share)

Critical Engine Trap

Root Cause & Structural Flaw

In data_manager.py (lines 58-68) and backtester.py (lines 68-75):

  • Pending stop loss orders can never execute (order.asset not in data: return None).
  • Strategy exit orders (self.close('STOCK_A')) are permanently ignored.
  • In analyzer.py, the position's value vanishes, showing an instant 100% loss of invested capital.

MAJ-01 Mark-to-Market Liquidity Discontinuity (Artificial Drawdown Spikes)
Major Analytics Bug

Root Cause & Structural Flaw

In analyzer.py (lines 64-70):

  • Portfolio equity is marked to market using: pos.size * data[pos.asset][4].
  • If an asset has no trades on bar ts (e.g. low liquidity, halt, circuit breaker), pos.asset not in data.
  • The position's valuation is completely omitted from positions_value for that bar!
  • No last-known-price (carry-forward) mechanism exists.

Quantitative & Risk Impact

For any portfolio holding illiquid equities, the equity curve suffers violent artificial flash-crashes to cash value, followed by immediate recoveries on the next tick.

This generates spurious 50% to 90% maximum drawdown figures and destroys the calculated Sharpe ratio, leading quant teams to reject valid strategies.

MAJ-02 Zero-Slippage & Infinite Liquidity in Default Execution Model
Major Microstructure Bias

Root Cause & Structural Flaw

In broker.py and execution_model.py:

  • Broker instantiates SimpleExecutionModel with DefaultSlippage by default.
  • DefaultSlippage returns price unaltered (0.00 bps slippage).
  • SimpleExecutionModel returns order.remaining_size without checking bar volume.

A strategy can trade $50,000,000 in a micro-cap stock on a 1-minute bar that traded only 500 shares. The entire size executes cleanly at the Open price.

MAJ-03 Limit Order Touch Execution (Zero Queue Delay) & STOPLIMIT Pathing
Major Execution Bias

Root Cause & Structural Flaw

In broker.py:

  • Limit Orders: Filled 100% whenever Low <= order.price (BUY) or High >= order.price (SELL). Merely touching the price level assumes 100% fill priority.
  • STOPLIMIT Orders: Checks if trigger price AND limit price fall inside the bar's high-low range, assuming the trigger occurred chronologically before the limit price was touched.

Quantitative & Risk Impact

In real exchange order books, an order placed after other participants sits at the back of the queue. If Low merely touches the limit price for 1 lot, an order for 10,000 shares would receive zero fills.

Mean-reversion and market-making strategies that place limit orders at support/resistance levels show massive fill-rate inflation.

Category 4: Performance Analytics & Statistical Fallacies (The "Inflated Scorecard" Trap)
MAJ-04 Intraday Sharpe Ratio 19.36x Deflation & Breakeven Trade Stripping
Major Statistical Flaw

Root Cause & Structural Flaw

In analyzer.py:

  • Sharpe Annualization: Multiplies per-bar returns by np.sqrt(252) regardless of frequency. For 1-minute bars (375 bars/day), the annualizing factor must be $\sqrt{252 \times 375} = \sqrt{94,500}$.
  • self.risk_free_rate is defined in __init__ but completely omitted from the calculation!
  • Win Rate Calculation: Filters closing_trades = [t for t in self.trades if t.pnl != 0]. This strips out all trades that closed at break-even ($0 PnL)!

Quantitative & Risk Impact

- Sharpe Ratio: An institutional intraday strategy with a true annualized Sharpe ratio of 2.5 will report a deflated Sharpe of 0.13 ($\sqrt{375} \approx 19.36\times$ deflation), causing viable alpha strategies to be discarded.

- Win Rate: If a strategy has 2 wins ($+100$), 2 losses ($-100$), and 6 breakeven exits ($0$), num_close_trades counts only 4. Win rate is reported as 50% instead of 20%!

Category 5: Lookahead & Forward Bias Vulnerabilities (Future Data Leakage)
FWD-01 Intra-Bar High/Low Execution Telepathy (Dual Target & Stop Fills)
FIXED & TESTED Parameter: intra_bar_fill_model (Default: POLARITY)

Root Cause & Structural Flaw

In broker.py:

  • When a position had both a Target Profit (LIMIT SELL at 110) and a Stop Loss (SL SELL at 90), and a volatile bar spanned High=115, Low=85:
  • The engine evaluated orders in dictionary insertion order.
  • If the target order was placed first, the engine filled the target at 110, and then immediately evaluated the stop-loss order on the same bar, filling it at 85 and accidentally flipping the position short!

Implemented Institutional Solution

Implemented a clean, explicit parameter intra_bar_fill_model="POLARITY" on Backtester and Broker:

  • "POLARITY" (Default — Trajectory Modeling): Reconstructs intra-bar price sequence from candle polarity & open gaps:
    • Open Gap takes priority (gap down below stop triggers SL immediately at Open).
    • Bullish bar (Close ≥ Open): Open → Low → High → Close (Low hit first → SL executes on Long).
    • Bearish bar (Close < Open): Open → High → Low → Close (High hit first → Target executes on Long).
  • "CONSERVATIVE" (Prudential Risk Standard): Stop Loss is unconditionally prioritized on ambiguous bars to avoid optimistic bias.
  • OCO Bracket Cancellation: Sibling exit order is immediately cancelled upon fill, eliminating accidental position flipping.
FWD-02 STOPLIMIT Sequence Lookahead (Pre-Trigger Dip Fill)
Critical Forward Bias

Root Cause & Structural Flaw

In broker.py (lines 196-206):

  • Checks if High >= trigger_price AND Low <= limit_price on the same bar.
  • If a breakout pullback order has Trigger=105 and Limit=102: if the bar opened at 100, dipped to 99, and then rallied to 106, the engine fills the order at 102.
  • However, the price of 102 occurred before the trigger of 105 was breached. The order was not active when 102 traded!

Quantitative & Risk Impact

Uses future intra-bar knowledge to execute limit orders that were not yet in the exchange book when the fill price occurred, guaranteeing impossible entry prices.

FWD-03 Reverse-Time History Slicing in Vectorized Indicators
High Risk Lookahead Trap

Root Cause & Structural Flaw

In history.py (lines 33-39):

  • BarHistory.__getitem__ unwraps slicing from newest to oldest: [Bar_T, Bar_{T-1}, Bar_{T-2}, ...].
  • Vectorized libraries (TA-Lib, NumPy, SciPy) assume arrays are chronological (index 0 = oldest).
  • When passed to TA-Lib or rolling filters, indicators calculate backwards in time: lagged indicators become leading filters, smoothing future bars into past values.

Quantitative & Risk Impact

Filters and rolling indicators inadvertently leak future price trends into current signals, giving strategies artificial predictive power.

FWD-04 Timezone Over-Conversion Shift (5.5-Hour Cross-Session Contamination)
High Risk Data Leak

Root Cause & Structural Flaw

In data_manager.py (lines 26-31):

  • Unconditionally assumes all naive CSV timestamps are UTC and calls .tz_localize('UTC').tz_convert('Asia/Kolkata') (+5.5 hours).
  • If historical CSVs are already recorded in local Indian Standard Time (IST), the 09:15 AM market open is shifted to 02:45 PM.
  • Mixing local and UTC data causes afternoon data to be evaluated during the morning session.

Quantitative & Risk Impact

A strategy running on shifted data sees price action hours before it occurred in real time, generating 100% lookahead bias across sessions.
